## Brand & Style

The design system is crafted for high-performance internal operations, combining corporate rigor with the warmth, tactile softness, and visual delight of modern studio productivity tools. Built specifically for an Indonesian corporate team managing daily work rhythms (Absensi, Tugas, Laporan Harian GMV, and Matriks WRM), it balances uncompromising operational discipline with an approachable, humane interface.

### Visual Character & Design Movement
The style blends **Soft Modern Minimalist SaaS** with **Tactile Pill Architecture**:
- **Canvas & Elevation**: Seamless, soothing off-white/warm slate backgrounds (`#F4F5F9`) layered with crisp milk-white cards (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Container Geometry**: Deeply curved card surfaces with organic 20px–28px outer radiuses, framed by featherweight translucent borders (`rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.06)`).
- **Segmented Controls & Tags**: Dark pill-shaped high-contrast filters juxtaposed with joyful pastel status backplates (soft amber, seafoam mint, lilac, and sky blue).
- **Tone of Voice**: Clear, direct, and motivating Bahasa Indonesia, delivering critical accountability milestones (e.g., *Absen Pulang*, *Laporan GMV*, *Matriks WRM: LANJUT / UBAH CARA*) without feeling punishing or cold.

## Colors

The color architecture is built around deep obsidian primary anchors (`#0F172A`), warm light-gray backdrop surfaces (`#F4F5F9`), and crisp white card containers. Visual interest and rapid categorical recognition are delivered through tinted pastel status containers and punchy semantic indicators.

### Semantic Color Logic
- **Primary (`#0F172A`)**: Reserved for primary actions, active pill navigation toggles, strong headlines, and high-emphasis controls.
- **Secondary (`#2563EB`)**: Accent blue used for links, interactive highlights, active progress bars, and operational data indicators.
- **Operational Status Indicators (Matriks WRM & Absensi)**:
  - **Hijau (`#10B981` / `#ECFDF5`)**: Used for target achieved, check-in valid, lead measure on track, and the `LANJUT` state.
  - **Merah (`#EF4444` / `#FEF2F2`)**: Highlights critical shortfall, overdue task, sequential failure (`merah_beruntun`), and the `UBAH CARA` / `ALARM` state.
  - **Orange (`#F97316` / `#FFF7ED`)**: Intermediate state, warning threshold, or `SABAR` state.
- **Pastel Category Tints**: Task and project cards leverage low-saturation background washes (`#EFF6FF` soft sky, `#FAF5FF` lavender, `#FFF7ED` warm apricot) to separate complex workflows into scannable clusters.

## Typography

Typography is powered exclusively by **Plus Jakarta Sans**, offering a contemporary geometric structure softened by humane grotesque terminals. It provides exceptional legibility for Indonesian team communications, financial GMV data entries, and dense operational tables.

### Typesetting Guidelines
- **Dashboard Headers**: Top-level views pair an understated subtitle (`body-sm` in `#64748B`) directly stacked on top of a bold title (`headline-lg` in `#0F172A`), creating immediate situational context.
- **Data Display**: Numeric values, currency strings (IDR/USD), and time logs use `metric-value` or `headline-md` with tabular numerals enabled to ensure vertical alignment across KPI cards.
- **Card Hierarchy**: Internal card headers use `headline-sm` (`16px`, weight 600), body descriptions use `body-sm` (`13px`), and metadata badges use `label-md` or `label-sm` in full pill containers.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a modular card-cluster architecture framed within an intentional peripheral padding system. Content does not spread wall-to-wall; rather, it sits inside high-comfort modules over the neutral canvas.

### Layout System
- **Desktop (>= 1200px)**: A 12-column dynamic grid with a slim left-hand icon docking bar (64px width), top app-bar with global time filter pills and profile summary, followed by an asymmetric 4-column widget dashboard layout:
  - Left panel (col-span-3): Quick tasks, active assignments, and sequential alerts.
  - Center panel (col-span-6): Projects, WRM performance charts, and GMV trends.
  - Right panel (col-span-3): Schedule meetings, daily ticket queues, and approval gates.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: A single-column vertical flow with sticky bottom-dock navigation for the 5 flat menus (*Beranda, Absensi, Tugas, Laporan Harian, GRD*), maintaining 16px screen margins and 12px gaps between interactive cards.
- **Inner Card Padding**: Standard cards feature `1.5rem` (`24px`) internal padding; compact summary items and nested sub-cards use `1rem` (`16px`).

## Elevation & Depth

The design system strictly avoids heavy, muddy drop shadows. Depth is achieved via **tonal layering**, **micro-surface contrast**, and **whisper-soft ambient diffusion**.

### Layering Rules
- **Base Canvas (Level 0)**: Background `#F4F5F9`.
- **Card Containers (Level 1)**: White `#FFFFFF` with a 1px crisp outline `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.05)` and a micro-diffused ambient shadow: `0 4px 20px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.02)`.
- **Floating Overlays & Menus (Level 2)**: White `#FFFFFF` with `0 12px 32px -4px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08), 0 4px 12px -2px rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.03)` with border `rgba(15, 23, 42, 0.08)`.
- **Nested Task Items & Badges**: Nested cards (e.g. project tiles) sit flat on top of the parent white card using low-saturation pastel surface fills (`#ECFDF5`, `#FFF7ED`, `#FAF5FF`) without shadows, achieving clear organizational grouping through tone alone.

## Shapes

The shape language is characterized by friendly, organic curvature that softens dense enterprise data while maintaining structural order.

### Radius Specifications
- **Dashboard Containers & Primary Cards**: `rounded-2xl` to `rounded-3xl` (20px – 24px) for major outer modules.
- **Nested Task Cards & Metric Units**: `rounded-xl` (12px – 16px).
- **Navigation Controls, Filters & Badges**: Full pill (`rounded-full` / 9999px) for segment toggles (*Hari Ini*, *Minggu Ini*, *Bulan Ini*), search inputs, status indicators, and user avatar chips.
- **Interactive Buttons & Input Fields**: `rounded-full` for compact icon buttons and primary pills; `rounded-xl` (12px) for form inputs and operational data grids.

## Components

### 1. Pill Segmented Switchers & Toggles
- **Default (Inactive)**: Pill container with `#FFFFFF` or transparent fill, subtle border `#E2E8F0`, typography `label-md` in `#64748B`.
- **Active**: Deep obsidian `#0F172A` background, pure white `#FFFFFF` typography, no border, subtle shadow.
- **Usage**: Time window toggles (*Hari Ini*, *Minggu Ini*, *Bulan Ini*, *Laporan*), task urgency switches.

### 2. Metric & Task Cards
- **Parent Container**: Pure white `#FFFFFF`, 24px radius, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, padding 20px. Includes a top title row with optional circular pill icon action (e.g. arrow-up-right or plus sign).
- **Nested Pastel Items**: Inner item blocks with 14px radius, 14px padding. Colors cycle contextually:
  - Soft Orange: `#FFF7ED` with icon indicator and orange status pill.
  - Soft Blue: `#EFF6FF` with blue highlight dot.
  - Soft Green: `#ECFDF5` with green verification badge.

### 3. Operational Matrix Badges (WRM Indicators)
- Badges use pill geometry (`rounded-full`) with uppercase tracking:
  - **LANJUT**: `#ECFDF5` background, `#047857` text, leading 6px solid green dot.
  - **SABAR**: `#FFF7ED` background, `#C2410C` text, leading 6px solid orange dot.
  - **ALARM / UBAH CARA**: `#FEF2F2` background, `#B91C1C` text, leading 6px solid red dot.
  - **Sequential Alert (`merah_beruntun`)**: High-contrast red fill `#EF4444`, white text `#FFFFFF`, animated subtle warning pulse.

### 4. Input Fields & Search Bars
- Search inputs are capsule-shaped (`rounded-full`), `#FFFFFF` background, height 44px, inner padding `0 18px`, border 1px solid `#E2E8F0`.
- Icon aligned left in `#94A3B8`, placeholder text `body-md` in `#94A3B8`. Focused ring: 2px `#2563EB` with 10% opacity wash.

### 5. Circular Action Buttons
- Utility buttons (add task, open drawer, expand card) use 32px or 36px circular frames (`rounded-full`), white background, 1px border `#E2E8F0`, centering a sharp icon in `#475569`. Hover shifts background to `#F8FAFC` and icon to `#0F172A`.

### 6. Attendance & Photo Verification Modules (Absensi)
- Floating camera preview with 16px corner radius, live coordinate stamp in bottom-left corner with 80% opacity dark glass pill.
- Locked clock-out state (*Absen Pulang* button) renders with disabled neutral fill `#E2E8F0`, locked padlock glyph, and tooltip helper *"Selesaikan Laporan GMV Harian untuk membuka tombol ini"*.
